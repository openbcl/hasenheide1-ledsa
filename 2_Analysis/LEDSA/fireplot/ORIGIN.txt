Summary of the conventions (docs/plotstyle.md of the template) that save_pdf/lint_figure check or the figures follow:
- text rendered by LaTeX with preamble.tex (newtx fonts, siunitx settings of the document class); all figure text
  (axis labels, tick labels, legend) at the body size 12 pt; figures at most the text width 150 mm
  (figsize_single), included without scaling;
- no figure or axes titles (the caption carries that information); short panel labels set with ax.text (here the
  column heads "t = ... s" and the row labels "F1 (n = ...)") are used as facet labels;
- axis labels "Quantity / unit", plain units with maths for exponents, no siunitx commands, ASCII only;
- one series scheme (SeriesScheme) for all figures: colour = group, line style/marker = subgroup (in the profile
  figures colour = test, line style = source; the LEM/SICK markers are set outside the scheme);
- legends next to or above the data, never over it;
- byte-deterministic PDFs: SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH fixed at import, font-subset tags derived from the glyphs.
